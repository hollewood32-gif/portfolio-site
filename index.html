About

I'm a U.S. Navy veteran. I started out as a nuclear equipment technician, where following procedures exactly, testing equipment before trusting it, and documenting everything were part of every shift. Since then I've trained as an electrical technician and a cyber security specialist, and now I build web software.

I bring the same discipline to code: every change goes through a pull request, automated checks and a staging deploy before it reaches production. My first project, Ragdoll Fling, is a browser game I took from an idea to a live site with a full CI/CD pipeline and an online leaderboard.
